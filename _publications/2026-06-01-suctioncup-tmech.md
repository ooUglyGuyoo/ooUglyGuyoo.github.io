We developed a miniaturized, vision-based suction cup that gives robotic manipulators a sense of touch — enabling autonomous alignment and the safe grasping of delicate, misaligned objects.

Here are our main contributions:

- **Miniaturization**: Shrank the system to a compact 22 × 40 mm footprint by integrating a micro-camera.
- **Simultaneous Multimodal Sensing**: Real-time detection of 3-axis pressing forces and contact orientation for closed-loop surface alignment.
- **Universal Calibration**: Our neural network applies universally to identically designed suction cups, eliminating the need for per-unit recalibration.
- **Validated sensing**: Reported pitch/roll MAE of 1.43°/1.45° and Fx/Fy/Fz MAE of 0.254/0.256/0.763 N, with demonstrations on small, delicate, misaligned and curved objects.
